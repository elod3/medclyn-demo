Downloaded 2026-09-21 via curl (no login, no node/npm). Kit zips extracted with
unzip; individual .glb files copied out and renamed. Nothing was re-exported
or re-textured — files are byte-identical to what shipped in the source zip
(or, for rack.glb and forklift.glb, byte-identical to what the source served).
